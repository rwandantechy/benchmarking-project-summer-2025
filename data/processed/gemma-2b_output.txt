**Supervised Machine Learning**

* **Goal:** Learn a mapping between input and output data.
* **Training Data:** Data with labeled features (input and output values).
* **Supervised Learner:** A model that learns from the labeled training data.
* **Types of Learners:**
    * Linear regression
    * Support vector machines (SVM)
    * Decision trees
* **Output:** A model that can be used to make predictions or classifications on new data.

**Unsupervised Machine Learning**

* **Goal:** Discover patterns and relationships in data without prior labels.
* **Training Data:** Data with no labeled features.
* **Unsupervised Learner:** A model that learns from the relationships and patterns in the data.
* **Types of Learners:**
    * K-means clustering
    * Hierarchical clustering
    * Self-organizing maps
* **Output:** A model that can be used to identify clusters, outliers, or other meaningful patterns in data.

**Example:**

**Supervised Learning:** Training a linear regression model on a dataset of sales data. The model learns to map the input (customer attributes) to the output (total sales amount).

**Unsupervised Learning:** Clustering a collection of customer profiles based on their purchase history and demographics. The model discovers hidden groups of customers with similar characteristics.
